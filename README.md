# Awesome AI Tools

## Video

- [Clipwing](https://clipwing.pro/) - A tool for cutting long videos into dozens of short clips.
- [PixaSocial Ai](https://pixasocial.ai) - Psychology-powered social content platform with Reel Factory (long video → TikTok/Reels/Shorts), Product UGC Studio, audience profiling, and multi-channel publishing.
- [Recast Studio](https://recast.studio) - AI powered podcast marketing assistant.
